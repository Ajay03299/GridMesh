# GridMesh — pitch numbers and slide guide

Numbers from the official 5-seed run on the team MacBook (commit 308c435). Source of truth:
[`outputs/RESULTS.md`](../outputs/RESULTS.md) — if the experiments are re-run, check it before
updating slides. Forecast error = RMSE as a share of installed capacity (0.064 = 6.4%).

## One-line pitch
GridMesh lets solar plants train a shared 30-minute-ahead forecaster **without sharing raw data**,
automatically sidelines plants with **broken sensors**, and turns forecast uncertainty into a
**smarter grid reserve**.

## Judging criteria → what to show
- **Relevance:** grid reliability with intermittent renewables. Better 30-minute solar forecasts mean
  less reserve for the same reliability; plant owners keep their data.
- **Impact:** one config value takes the system from 4 to 100 plants; a config switch takes real plant
  data (measured power + a site-ID column); the README roadmap shows the path to a real deployment.
- **Innovation:** federated learning itself is not new. GridMesh adds reliability-aware aggregation
  (trust × data quality), event-aware participation and forecast-aware reserve sizing, and evaluates
  them honestly against FedAvg, local-only and centralized training.
- **Complexity & implementation:** PyTorch federated simulation, fault injection, drift detection,
  reserve simulator, live dashboard with a plain-English summary per scenario, 21 automated checks.
- **Schneider themes:** *efficiency* — less reserve per unit of reliability, half the traffic;
  *sustainability* — reliable forecasts make it easier to integrate more solar;
  *accessibility* — small plant owners can join without handing over raw data; the dashboard is
  readable by non-experts; *scalability* — 4 → 100 plants with the same code.

## Say these before the judges ask
- Weather and irradiance are **real**; PV power is **modeled** from them; plants and faults are
  **simulated**; demand is **synthetic**; cost coefficients are **assumptions**.
- Raw data never leaves a plant, but **secure aggregation / differential privacy are not implemented yet**.
- The n-sigma reserve covers 91.8% of intervals against a 95% target. The audit shows the rule is
  calibrated on textbook (Gaussian) errors, so the gap comes from real weather: errors cluster on
  cloudy days.
- Trained on January–September and tested on November–December, every learned model loses to smart
  persistence. Drift-triggered retraining is the next step.
- Event-aware participation trades about 1% accuracy for half the communication.
